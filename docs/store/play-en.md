# INCI Scan — Google Play (English)

**Short description (80 characters max)**  
Scan a cosmetic barcode or INCI list. Offline hazard ratings, no account.

**Full description**

INCI Scan helps you decide whether a cosmetic is suitable for you — at the shelf, with airplane mode on.

- Scan an EAN/UPC barcode and look up the product in the on-device catalog.
- If the barcode is missing, photograph the printed INCI list (skład) or paste it. You review every name before scoring.
- Ratings come from a local ingredient knowledge base (EU annex-inspired levels plus short shopper comments), not from the cloud.
- Set personal filters: fragrance-free, pregnancy caution, and ingredients you want to avoid.
- Leave-on vs rinse-off changes some scores (for example MIT in a cream vs a shampoo).
- Ads are optional banners on browse screens only. They never appear on the camera or the confirm-ingredients step.

This is an informational consumer tool. It is not a medical device and it is not a substitute for the ingredient list, a dermatologist, or official EU annexes.
