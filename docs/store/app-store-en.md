# INCI Scan — App Store (English)

**Subtitle (30 characters)**  
Offline cosmetic INCI scanner

**Promotional text**  
Look up a barcode or the printed ingredient list without an account. Ratings stay on your phone.

**Description**

INCI Scan is an offline cosmetic ingredient scanner for shoppers.

Scan a barcode, or capture the INCI / skład block when the product is not in the catalog. Confirm OCR names, then see a traffic-light rating that is not colour-only: each level has a shape, a word, and a colour.

Personal filters (fragrance-free, pregnancy caution, EU-labelled allergens, children caution, alcohol in leave-on, essential-oil cluster, avoid-list) can mark a product as not suitable for you even when the general rating is moderate. If usage is unknown, pick leave-on or rinse-off on the result screen.

Save products to a shelf, compare scans, and share a result as text or a simple image. Microplastics and animal-derived tags, when the catalog has them, are separate chips — not extra traffic-light colours. Sun-caution, children, and pregnancy notes are incomplete lists, not diagnoses. Wipe personal data on this device from Settings. There is no account.

Banner ads never cover the camera or the evaluate action. Consent (ATT) is required before personalized ads.

Not a medical device. Not a substitute for the pack, a dermatologist, or EU annexes.
